My Favorite City

My favorite city is Abuja, the capital city of Nigeria, centrally located within the Federal Capital Territory (FCT). Purpose-built in the 1980s to replace the congested coastal city of Lagos, it officially became the nation's political and administrative capital on December 12, 1991. Today, it stands as one of Africa's fastest-growing cities, renowned for its organized, modern layout, wide expressways, and distinct geographical landmarks.
